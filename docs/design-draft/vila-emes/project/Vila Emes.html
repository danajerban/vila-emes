Our story

A small house, kept by one family.

— since 1998

Vila Emes is a small hotel a short walk from the Adriatic. Three generations of our family look after the rooms, the breakfast table, and the little garden out back — and we've done so since the doors first opened in 1998.

Mornings start with coffee on the terrace and the smell of warm bread from the pastry shop just downstairs. Afternoons are unhurried. Shaban — our owner — will reserve a parking spot for you (sometimes with a chair), point you to the quiet stretch of beach, and is awake at 3am to meet you if your flight lands late.

We keep things simple: clean rooms, honest prices, and the kind of welcome you only get from a house run by people who live in it. You arrive a guest. You leave family.

— with love, the Emes family

Before you ask

Frequently asked

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is 12:00 – 18:00, check-out is 07:00 – 11:00. The front desk is staffed 24 hours, so if your flight lands late just let us know and we'll wait up.

Is breakfast included?

Breakfast isn't included in the room rate, but the pastry shop on the ground floor opens early and does excellent espresso, cappuccino, and fresh bakllava. Most guests start their day there.

Do you have parking?

Yes — free public parking right out front. With a little luck Shaban will hold a spot for you (sometimes with a chair).

Are pets welcome?

We're sorry — pets aren't allowed at Vila Emes.

Can children stay? Cribs and extra beds?

Children of all ages are welcome. Cribs aren't available, but an extra bed can be added on request for €5 per person, per night.

What languages do you speak?

Albanian, Italian, and English — between Shaban and his daughter you'll always find someone who can help.
