# Hotel Vila Emes

## About this property

**Comfortable Accommodations:** Hotel Vila Emes in Durrës offers family rooms with air-conditioning, private bathrooms, and free WiFi. Each room includes a refrigerator, seating area, and free toiletries.

**Exceptional Facilities:** Guests can enjoy a bar, coffee shop, and outdoor seating area. Additional amenities include a terrace, balcony, washing machine, and work desk. Free on-site parking is available.

**Prime Location:** Located 22 mi from Tirana International Airport, the hotel is an 8-minute walk from Durres Beach. Nearby attractions include Rock of Kavaje (3.7 mi) and Durres Amphiteatre (3 mi).

**Guest Satisfaction:** Highly rated for its attentive staff, convenient location, and beach access.

Couples in particular like the location – they rated it 9.5 for a two-person trip.
